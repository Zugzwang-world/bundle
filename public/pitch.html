ZW-FS-001 · a feature proposal for Claude · the Zugzwang Foundation

Bundle.

A toggle on Claude's chat index. Off, nothing changes. On, the chats you keep coming back to gather themselves.

Working prototype: zugzwang-claude-bundle.in/prototype

An independent proposal. The Zugzwang Foundation is not affiliated with, commissioned by, or endorsed by Anthropic. Interface depictions are illustrative reconstructions.

01 · the problem

Ongoing concerns scatter across a chronological list.

Meera has talked to Claude about her retirement money nine times over four months. Those chats are interleaved with a flat hunt, Spanish lessons, a recipe and a birthday message.

The list is honest about time and silent about what she keeps returning to. Search finds one chat. Projects need her to file things first. Neither shows her the thread.

02 · the idea

One toggle. Bundles above the list. Nothing beneath it moves.

Bundle chats
  • Scattered chats about the same concern gather into named, collapsible sections marked ✦.
  • A bundle is a view, not a move. Every chat stays exactly where it was.
  • Rename, remove a chat, hide a bundle, undo. Switch it off and the previous screen is back, unchanged.

03 · four invariants

What holds it together.

INV-1

The list is never replaced

Bundles sit above the chronological list. It is always there, always complete.

INV-2

A bundle never moves a chat

Every chat appears exactly once. Losing one is the named failure.

INV-3

Every name is correctable and safe

A health scare renders as Health. Never a condition, never a person.

INV-4

Bundle sees only what memory sees

Memory off, no bundles. No new data path, no new consent.

04 · the demo · four beats

Watch it, then watch how.

BEAT 1

A real chat

Type a message, get a reply. Claude writes the chat's card: a title and a two-line summary, live.

BEAT 2

Flip the toggle

The list never blinks. Skeletons appear above it. Three bundles form, and the new chat sits in the right one.

BEAT 3

An ambiguous chat

"How do I say 'the landlord raised the rent' in Spanish?" Claude picks a bundle and shows the runner-up and the reason.

BEAT 4

Open the hood

The stage view: each technique running on the right, one plain sentence per stage, real output on every card.

05 · under the hood

Five techniques. Claude only where judgement is needed.

cards ──► embed ──► map + cluster ──► rules ──► name ──► gate ──► merge ──► render ▲ └── new chat while on ──► attach ──► tie-break? ───────┘ code, in the browser: embeddings · cosine similarity · agglomerative clustering formation rules (≥ 4 chats · ≥ 2 days · nameable) · stability merge Claude, via the API: card · name · safety gate · tie-break

Two runs on the same chats give the same groups. Names freeze after their first render. Your corrections outrank the model.

06 · the safety gate

Name the domain, never the struggle.

  • Layer 1, code: a lexicon of conditions, medications and symptoms, and a person-name heuristic. A hit downgrades the name to its domain word.
  • Layer 2, Claude: pass, downgrade, or refuse, with a one-sentence reason.
  • A cluster of health-scare chats renders as Health. "Miscellaneous" is not a name; the model refuses instead.
